ShapeMod - installing on your Mac
==================================

1. Copy both .vst3 files into this folder (create it if it's missing):

     ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3

   In Finder: Go menu > Go to Folder... and paste the path above.

2. macOS blocks plugins downloaded from the internet. Open Terminal and paste:

     xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/ShapeMod*

3. In Ableton Live 12: Settings > Plug-Ins > turn on "Use VST3 Plug-in System Folders",
   then click Rescan. ShapeMod appears under Plug-Ins > dgussmann.

Which one to use
----------------
ShapeMod Instrument  Put it on a MIDI track in place of a synth. Load your synth inside it.
                     Notes from the track are passed through to the synth.
ShapeMod FX          Put it on any track's effect chain. Load an effect inside it.
